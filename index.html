Energy-aware hierarchical formation control of multi-UAV for persistent urban target tracking

Supplementary 3D Trajectory Comparison Animations  |  GitHub Repository
Paradigm 1 — Continuous Trailing Platform (follow/): A heavy UGV carrying a single rooftop battery-swapping pad trails the target car along its route at a standoff distance of 25 m. Closed-loop 600 s mission trials at 4.0 C discharge rate with shared safety reserve Tres = 60 s.
Paradigm 2 — Distributed Fixed Swapping Stations (pads/): Four stationary UGV battery-swapping pads are deployed at periphery locations across the Town10 road network (mean nearest-pad distance 51 m, 95th percentile 94 m). Departing UAVs select the station minimizing predicted 3D flight time plus queue wait time and execute hierarchical 3D return/rejoin trajectories.
